One output, many owners

Everyone shares one coin. Anyone can leave in one transaction.

Balances live in an off-chain tree of sums, and one on-chain output commits to its root. To leave, you show your leaf and its proof to the output itself. The script checks it against the root and pays you, with no timelock and no one else's signature.

Components 6 ITEMS

  • C-01TREE

    Join

    Add a leaf under the committed root and recommit the new one.

  • C-021 TX

    Exit

    Present a leaf and its proof to the output. It pays you and keeps the rest.

  • C-03TREE

    Pay

    Move balance from one leaf to another. The sums stay consistent.

  • C-04TREE

    Swap

    Exchange one leaf's balance for another's in a single update.

  • C-051 TX

    Evict

    The operator removes an unresponsive leaf and pays it out on-chain.

  • C-06TREE

    Batch

    Fold many updates into one new root.
