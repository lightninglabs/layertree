One coin, many owners

Share one output. Leave in one step.

Many people's balances live in a single Bitcoin output. An off-chain tree keeps the sums, and the output's script checks your proof against the committed root. So you can leave in one transaction, with no waiting period, and the operator can never take or freeze your money.

Six things the output can do

All of them are one machine: prove a node against the current root, then fold a new node up the same path.

Join

Graft a new member, or a whole prebuilt group, into an empty slot.
