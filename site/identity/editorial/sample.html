A shared-UTXO design

One output, many owners, anyone may leave.

Balances live in an off-chain tree of sums, and a single Bitcoin output commits to its root. To leave, you hand the output your leaf and its proof; the script checks them against the root and recreates the output under the next one. One transaction, no timelock, and the operator holds no key that can take or freeze your balance.

Six moves, one machine

§ 7
  • i.

    Join

    The operator grafts a new member, or a whole prebuilt subtree, into an empty slot.

    operator
  • ii.

    Exit

    Present your leaf and proof; one transaction pays your balance to your exit key.

    leaf owner
  • iii.

    Pay

    Channel leaves forward payments between members without touching the tree.

    any two leaves
  • iv.

    Swap

    Hash-locked vouchers trade tree balance for Lightning sats, atomically.

    buyer and seller
  • v.

    Evict

    After the lease height, anyone holding the evictor key removes a leaf and pays its owner exactly.

    evictor
  • vi.

    Batch

    Several exits, or a whole expired cohort, share one transaction and one fee.

    same machine, repeated

LayerTree is a design with a reference implementation. The opcodes it relies on are proposals, not consensus.
