\section{Trust Model}\label{sec:trust}

Custody never rests with the operator, given a taptree whose $m$ the
member has recomputed (\S\ref{sec:output}). The key path is unspendable,
and every script path needs a proof against the committed root plus the
affected owner's signature, the evictor's after the lease (paying the
leaf in full), or the operator's for operations that only add value or
move a cohort intact.
Table~\ref{tab:trust} (Appendix~\ref{app:ops}) lists each party's powers: the operator can refuse to co-sign, graft, evict after a lease (paying in full) and detach a cohort, but cannot move or reduce another leaf, delay an exit beyond [C] or freeze value it did not graft; an owner can always exit, and anyone can redeem a valid voucher or relay a transition.



\paragraph{What a member does.} A member follows every spend of the tree
output and updates its own path from the witness alone (spec~9.7),
verifies its path before funding a join (Algorithm~\ref{alg:accept}) and
the source before accepting a voucher (\S\ref{sec:holder-risk}), keeps a
confirmed fee coin for every unilateral path but FULL's fee-from-payout
exit (Appendix~\ref{app:impl}), and must be online to exit unless a tower
holds its $\tr{EXIT}^{t}$ authorisation (Algorithm~\ref{alg:exit}).

\paragraph{Liveness.}\label{sec:liveness} Every operation spends and
recreates one output, so an honest exit can be overtaken by conflicting
spends (a corrupt member alone can carve its balance back and forth),
and its delay depends on policy and fees. We state it as an assumption.
\emph{[C] (contention):} at most $\kappa \ge 1$ spends of a tree output
confirm per block, and while an honest spend is pending at the
prevailing feerate at most $\beta$ conflicting adversarial spends
confirm ahead of it. $\kappa$ is mempool policy (25 under Bitcoin Core's
chain limits before version 31, at most 64 under its cluster limit
since~\cite{corepolicy,clustermempool}, as low as 1 once anyone hangs
descendants off a payout); $\beta$ is the adversary's fee budget against
the honest bid. Under [C] an honest spend with $q$ others queued ahead
confirms within $\lat(q) = \lat_L(\lceil (\beta + q)/\kappa \rceil + 1)$
blocks, $\lat_L$ being the chain's own delay. We use $\kappa = 25$, the
lowest limit relay peers may enforce (spec 10.5): cooperatively
10{,}000 leaves leaving at once take 400 blocks (157 at 64), so a tree
is sized by exit throughput, not depth. The operator
can evict any leaf once its lease passes, so a member who wants a longer
guarantee needs a later lease at entry; no carve extends one.
