\section{What Is Proved}\label{sec:proved}

The rules of every operation are mechanised in Lean~4~\cite{lean4}
(about 1{,}900 lines, no Mathlib), with an axiom audit of
\LeanTheoremCount{} headline theorems, none resting on an unproved step.
They show that a root binds its tree, so a leaf whose path verifies is
the leaf at that position; that every sequence of steps conserves the
total, with no hash assumption; that every known leaf can be paid out by
its honest witness; that a graft without a real preimage freezes only
the value it brought; that a voucher cannot be replayed and a carve
keeps the evictor, lease and dust floor; and that the cohort operations
and encodings behave as the scripts assume. Sums are conserved
unconditionally, but an exit is guaranteed only to a member who verified
its path. Scripts are matched to the rules by executable checks
(\S\ref{sec:eval}), not a verified compiler.

For each tier, the protocol UC-realises an ideal functionality of the
shared output in a global-ledger hybrid model~\cite{canettiUC,bmtz17},
with idealised hashes, taproot binding in the random-oracle model and
[C], against a static adversary corrupting the operator and any members.
The full version gives the functionality, the simulator, the proof and
the Lean statements.
