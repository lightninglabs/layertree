\paragraph{Deadlines and conditions.} A channel leaf's HTLC deadlines add the counterparty's stale updates to the exit's confirmation time: with $\mathsf{csv\_delay} = 144$ the invoice's minimum final delta is about 220 blocks, against lnd's default of 80~\cite{lnd}, and about 1{,}775 channel leaves may close in one window (Appendix~\ref{app:impl-chan}). A channel leaf has exact payouts, so the owner
cannot underfund the channel; $\mathsf{seq} = \mathsf{SEQ\_FINAL}$, so no
voucher or \tr{SPLIT} can carve the capacity the signed states commit
to; and lease zero with the counterparty as evictor, so eviction is the
counterparty's force-close. The state machine and the budget run end to
end in the simulator with two blocks of margin; a proof
that channel leaves compose is open. A force-close costs
$\approx 1{,}681\vB$ (its exit creates the funding output), against
$684\vB$ for a simple taproot channel's open plus force close~\cite{taprootchans}.
